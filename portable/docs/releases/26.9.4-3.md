- Windows keep the display you left them on, even after a launch without it

---

- A window remembered on a second display could come back on the built-in
  screen and forget where it belonged. Restoring the exact frame was fixed in
  26.9.4-2, but not the case that actually loses it: launching while that
  display is asleep or unplugged. The window was moved onto the built-in screen
  to keep it reachable — correct — and then that stand-in position was saved
  over the real one on quit, so a single launch without the display was enough
  to forget it permanently.
- A window whose display is missing is still shown somewhere visible, now
  centred on the main screen rather than pinned to its corner, but that
  placement is no longer written to the session. The position you left is kept
  until you actually move or resize the window, and if the display reappears
  while the app is running the window returns to it.
