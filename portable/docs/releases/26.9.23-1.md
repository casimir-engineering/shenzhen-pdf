- Keep local document copies and version history with Collection
- Compare PDF and Markdown revisions, and recover missing originals
- Organize persistent colored tab groups and search everything with Cmd+K

---

- **Collection and history (macOS).** Choose at first use whether to keep local, read-only copies and history. Continue opening originals normally. Browse earlier versions and kept milestones in the reader’s persistent History sidebar tab, with a Latest badge on the newest version. Configure exclusions, storage limits and location in Collection. Turning capture off preserves existing history.
- **Collection browser refinement (macOS).** Integrated search shows page thumbnails and highlighted context; clicking a match opens its saved page in the reader. History opens the latest document with the reader’s History tab selected. Readable encrypted PDFs receive previews too. Documents and Settings share a left sidebar. Collection opens as a separate app so Cmd+Tab switches between it and the reader. Storage defaults to unlimited; optional cleanup prunes old versions of least-opened documents before final copies, while Keep protects an entire history. Search, history and browsing positions persist. Collection controls align symbols with labels and preserve rounded outlines during search editing. Documents and thumbnails always show one latest saved copy per document. Earlier versions and the Latest pill are confined to History. Opening Collection also handles excluded documents and failed initial captures without crashing during date sorting.
- **Compare revisions.** Inspect PDF and Markdown versions side by side. Removed content is red, additions are green, and change markers help navigate text, images and page changes. Scrolling and zoom can stay linked or be controlled independently.
- **Recover documents.** Missing originals retain their tabs. Open a dated archived copy, save a separate editable copy, or locate the original manually or through exact-content matching. Opening a uniquely matching file automatically reconnects a lost original while preserving its history. Older saved copies show a dated toolbar pill; when the original is missing, click the pill to locate it.
- **Search and return.** Cmd+K searches open document names, groups, open text and Collection. Use `col:` for Collection alone. Cmd+Backspace returns to the previous active document, reopening it with its position if necessary; press again to switch back.
- **Tab groups.** Create, rename, recolor and collapse groups. Rounded tinted boundaries identify membership, and stronger selected tabs keep the same readable title color as other tabs. Drag a group's left handle to reorder it or move it to another window. Groups and reading positions survive relaunch. Dragged tabs follow the pointer while neighboring tabs make room inside their group. In crowded windows, General tabs yield space to custom groups while the selected document stays visible. Newly created groups stay on the side where you create them, including after relaunch. Labels are centered; click a collapsed group to open its last-used document, and use the right-click menu to rename it.
- **Markdown layout.** Tables keep short columns readable. Choose paper size, orientation, margins and explicit page breaks in front matter; page-number footers also appear in exports.
- **Markdown sidebar.** Unsupported Comments tabs are hidden, and comment commands stay disabled in Markdown menus.
- **Markdown editing.** Open a source in your preferred editor from its tab or page menu. The chosen editor is remembered and can be changed in Settings.
- **Copy the intended page.** Right-click Copy Page and Copy Page Image use the page under the pointer.
- **Optional agent access.** The native CLI and MCP adapter inspect PDF and Markdown, export previews, report layout and open a page or highlighted passage. They run only when invoked; see [setup and commands](../agent-interface.md).
